- **S10-07**：[Royal Society referee report RR/1/62](https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/rr_1_62/referees-report-by-samuel-hunter-christie-and-john-bostock-on-a-paper-experimental-researches-in-electricity-by-michael-faraday)。性质：学会一手审稿馆藏记录；已核内容／边界：审稿报告日期1832年3月5日、推荐刊出；未逐页读15页手稿。
